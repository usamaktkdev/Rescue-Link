RescueLink
RescueLink is a lightweight emergency assistance application designed for areas where professional emergency services may not always arrive quickly. The application allows nearby people to help one another during emergencies by combining live SOS alerts, volunteer assistance, and hazard reporting.  

Core Features:

    Offline-Resilient SOS: If the internet drops, the app stores essential emergency data locally and continuously retries transmission until the connection returns.  

    Volunteer Coordination: Users can enable Volunteer Mode to receive full-screen SOS alerts, accept incidents, and broadcast their estimated time of arrival to the person in distress.  

    Community Hazard Mapping: Users can report dangerous locations (like landslides or flooded roads) with compressed images, alerting anyone within a 15 km radius.  

    Low-Bandwidth Optimization: Built for rural and low-connectivity environments, the app prioritizes small data transfers, cached map data, and lightweight location updates.  

    Fail-Safe Emergency Contacts: Optionally sends a standard SMS with coordinates to predefined contacts, ensuring alerts are delivered even if the recipient does not have the app installed.
